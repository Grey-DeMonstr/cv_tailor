# Voice

How the owner writes. `get-cv` reads this before every cover letter. It decides how sentences
sound; it never overrides the rules in the skill's `cover-letter-style.md`.

- TODO(ask): Which language are your applications usually written in?

## Register

Defaults, until replaced by a description of the owner's own writing:

- First person, plain words, no jargon.
- Short declarative sentences.
- Concrete systems and numbers instead of adjectives.

## Samples

Two or three short excerpts the owner wrote themselves, verbatim, each with its source. Register
only: they show how sentences sound, not how a letter is built.

## Habits and punctuation

Defaults, until replaced:

- Contractions are fine.
- No em dashes and no semicolons.

## Never write

Phrases and framings the owner does not want in anything sent under their name.

## Motivations that exclude each other

Pairs of motivations from `stories.md` that cannot appear in the same letter.
