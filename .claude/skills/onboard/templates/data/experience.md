# Experience

Reverse chronological. One `##` per employer with its overall dates. One `###` per role when the
title changed inside the same employer. Dates are written "Month Year – Month Year".

## Gaps

Any period longer than three months between roles, with what happened in it. Its dates are the
empty months: a role ending October 2020 and the next starting March 2021 leave a gap of
"November 2020 – February 2021".

## Cross-cutting facts

Facts that span several roles: total years in a field, the largest team ever led.
