# Stories and motivations

Raw material for cover letters. Each entry is a `###` heading under one of the sections below and
a few true sentences. Letters are written fresh from these and never copy an entry wholesale.

## Motivations

Why the owner is looking, and what they want the next job to be. One `###` entry per motivation.

## Anecdotes

One thing that happened, in context, with its outcome. One `###` entry each.

## Personal

Facts about the owner as a person that they are happy to mention in a letter.
