a few true sentences. Letters are written fresh from these and never copy an entry wholesale.

Why the owner is looking, and what they want the next job to be. One `###` entry per motivation.

## Anecdotes

One thing that happened, in context, with its outcome. One `###` entry each.

### A late tracking update is a driver waiting at a gate (source: letter.txt)
At Larkfield Freight, a late shipment-tracking update means a driver waiting at a gate. This is
how Sam explains why latency in the tracking system matters. The measured results that go with it
are in `experience.md` (rate-calculation p95 900 ms to 120 ms; nightly report 50 minutes to 4).
