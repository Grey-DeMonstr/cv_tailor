# Voice

How the owner writes. `get-cv` reads this before every cover letter. It decides how sentences
sound; it never overrides the rules in the skill's `cover-letter-style.md`.

## Language

- English (source: user, September 2026)

## Register

Described from the one sample, `sources/letter.txt`:

- First person, plain words, talks to the reader directly ("Hi," at the top, "Thanks for reading."
  at the end, signs off with first name only).
- Paragraphs of one or two sentences. The whole letter is four short paragraphs.
- Makes a point with one concrete picture instead of a claim: a driver waiting at a gate stands
  for why latency matters.
- Says openly what it is not doing: "I don't have a long list of buzzwords for you." Offers to
  explain on a call rather than listing more.

## Samples

Two or three short excerpts the owner wrote themselves, verbatim, each with its source. Register
only: they show how sentences sound, not how a letter is built.

- "I like work where a slow query is somebody's actual problem. At Larkfield a late tracking update
  means a driver waiting at a gate. That's the kind of thing I want to keep fixing."
  (source: sources/letter.txt)
- "I don't have a long list of buzzwords for you. I can tell you what I built and what it changed,
  and I'm happy to go through any of it on a call." (source: sources/letter.txt)

## Habits and punctuation

From `sources/letter.txt`:

- Contractions throughout: "I'm", "I've", "That's", "I don't".
- Sentences are short to medium, mostly one main clause, the longest about 25 words.
- Commas and full stops only. No em dashes, no semicolons, no exclamation marks, no bullet lists.
- Refers to the employer by its short name ("Larkfield"), not its full legal name.

## Never write

Phrases and framings the owner does not want in anything sent under their name.

- "passionate" (source: user, September 2026)
- "results-driven" (source: user, September 2026)

## Motivations that exclude each other

Pairs of motivations from `stories.md` that cannot appear in the same letter.

- None. The two motivations recorded in September 2026 (reliability that matters to real people;
  leaving because Larkfield is freezing its platform) can honestly sit in one letter.
