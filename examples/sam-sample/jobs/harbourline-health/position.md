Senior Backend Engineer — Harbourline Health
Remote (UK) · Full-time
https://harbourline.example/careers/senior-backend-engineer

Harbourline Health is a fast-paced, world-class team building booking software for independent
clinics.

What you'll do
- Own the appointment-booking API used by 300 clinics and bring its p95 response time under 200 ms.
- Fix the scheduling database: the appointments table has outgrown its design and reports time out.
- Run on-call for services on Kubernetes and improve how we handle incidents.
- Mentor two mid-level engineers.

What we're looking for
- 5+ years building backend services in Python or Go.
- Deep PostgreSQL experience with large tables.
- Kubernetes in production.
- Experience leading a small team.
- Nice to have: healthcare data or clinical systems.

How to apply
Fill in the form on our careers page. It asks for your name, email and phone, a CV and a short
cover letter.
